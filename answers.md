# Answers — Animators Hive Hub Project Questions

## About what you built

**1. Looking back, did your website include everything users really need? Was anything missing?**

The website included everything users really need, but there are things that would be nice to add to it.

**2. You didn't get to ask any animators what they wanted. Do you think that changed what you built?**

It didn't. Nonetheless, I wish I did, so that it could stand out more from other websites with the objectives what I built has.

**3. In your proposal you said users could report bad content and an admin could remove it. That wasn't built. What happened?**

I forgot to add that feature.

## About your design choices

**4. You moved from React and Supabase to SvelteKit and Cloudflare. Why did you switch? Are you glad you did?**

SvelteKit is faster than React. Cloufare would make running the website faster, compared to using Supabase, while hosting it. I don't think Supabase has semantic search either, for looking up things on the website easier.

**5. Which part of your design are you least happy with? Why?**

None. I just wish I did more for it.

**6. If you started again tomorrow, what would you design differently?**

A checker to see for whether an animation is A.I generated, in the upload process, before the video is made live.

## About building and testing

**7. Which feature gave you the most trouble to build? What made it hard?**

I would say it was video uploading section. This is because I didn't see myself dealing with relational databases only. I had to work with big data types too.

**8. One test didn't pass: the website didn't always block someone after 30 sign-in attempts. Why might that matter?**

That will matter, especially with identifying the user making sign-ins as an intruder, because at this stage a level of security will not be in place.

**9. Do you feel you tested your website enough? What else would you test?**

Yes, I feel I've tested the website enough.

## About your plan and time

**10. Your plan was to work from May to September, but you started in late September. What held you back?**

The work was started months before late September. I had to go through it again because I didn't do it properly.

**11. How did starting late affect your project?**

I didn't start late. I just completed the work properly late. It didn't affect my project, nonetheless.

**12. While working, did you check how you were doing against your plan? Why or why not?**

I checked how I was doing against my plan. I did that because I couldn't guarrantee completing the goals of my project if I didn't.

## About what you learned

**13. In your own words, what did you set out to do, and what did you end up with?**

I set out to create a website to promote animators, so that A.I animations won't put overshadow their work. What I ended up with was a website with that objective, but an interface for users to chat with, and an A.I checker for animated videos missing.

**14. What was the hardest moment for you during the project?**

Following the key objective of the website for the project while creating it.

**15. Your first version and its data were lost. What did that teach you?**

It taught me to back-up my work more often, and be more responsible for it.

**16. What did you learn about planning and managing your time?**

I learnt that time is always free to use for anything. The more you work with your time, the more you are alone. As you are in solitude, your maturity grows to create massive things within any timeframe.

**17. Which skill do you feel you improved the most?**

My skill for writing.

**18. Name three things you'll do differently in your next project.**

1. A chatting interface for users to communicate with.
2. An A.I checker to see whether uploaded videos are made with A.I
3. Beginner video tutorials for animating.
